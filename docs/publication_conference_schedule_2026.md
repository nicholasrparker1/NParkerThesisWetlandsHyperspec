# 2026 Publication and Conference Schedule

## Fixed Milestones

- Publication submission: November 2026
- Conference presentation: December 2026
- Internal analysis freeze: October 15, 2026
- Complete manuscript draft to advisor: October 23, 2026

## August 19-31: Freeze the Reference Framework

- finalize KSSL database, MIR replicate, unit, and provenance documentation
- finalize Montana-North Dakota SSURGO/NWI evidence table and version-0 diagnostic
- define the tool interface, score/confidence distinction, and validation rules
- identify the exact airborne dataset, acquisition dates, coverage, and access path
- identify available field-confirmed hydric indicators or explicitly bound the claim

Deliverable: reproducible reference dataset, current results deck, and locked methods outline.

## September 1-30: Build the Airborne Bridge

- spatially match eligible KSSL/field locations to airborne VNIR-SWIR observations
- screen vegetation, water, shadow, mixed pixels, and exposed-soil usability
- estimate or test transferable soil-property signals in airborne spectra
- compare airborne results with laboratory MIR-derived properties
- run project/site/geographic validation and uncertainty diagnostics

Deliverable: final primary analysis tables and first publication figures.

Decision gate on September 15: if suitable airborne overlap is inadequate, narrow
the paper to a laboratory-to-spatial evidence framework and present the airborne
connection as the validated next-stage tool architecture rather than an achieved
delineation result.

## October 1-15: Final Analysis

- resolve sensitivity analyses and reviewer-style checks
- finalize figure set, captions, methods, and limitations
- freeze data, code version, and numerical results on October 15

Deliverable: final results package and reproducible run manifest.

## October 16-31: Manuscript

- complete full draft by October 23
- advisor review and revision during the final week of October
- prepare supplement, data/code availability statement, and repository documentation

Deliverable: submission-ready manuscript before November begins.

## November: Submit and Convert to Conference Story

- submit the paper before the external deadline, retaining buffer for portal issues
- convert manuscript figures into a concise conference narrative
- prepare backup slides for methods, validation, and limitations

## December: Conference Delivery

- rehearse timing and questions
- emphasize the tool architecture, validated achievements, and remaining airborne gap
- avoid presenting mapped evidence as regulatory or field-confirmed hydric status

## Critical Risks

1. No suitable airborne overlap or acquisition metadata.
2. No independent field-confirmed hydric validation set.
3. Geographic non-transfer of the mapped-evidence association.
4. Excessive scope expansion after the October analysis freeze.

The first two risks determine the final paper claim and must be resolved or bounded
by September 15.
